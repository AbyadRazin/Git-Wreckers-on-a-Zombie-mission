What the numbers say
--------------------
- Every scenario's run number i starts from the same layout, so the four
  rows are compared on equal starting positions. "+/-" is one standard
  error: two averages only really differ if they are several of these apart.
- Medics clearly help: 9.67 vs 8.71 survivors, about 4 standard errors apart.
- Traps on their own barely change survivors (8.93 vs 8.71) but do leave
  fewer zombies at the end (2.06 vs 2.53).
- Medics and traps together are the best setup: 10.23 survivors, about
  1.5 more than with neither, more than 6 standard errors apart.
- The split by trap activity is a correlation, not a cause: runs where
  traps caught something had FEWER survivors. Traps catch more when there
  are more zombies around, and more zombies means fewer survivors. The
  fair test of whether traps help is the paired comparison above, not
  this split.
